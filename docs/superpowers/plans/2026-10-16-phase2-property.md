### Task 1: Property model (BudgetCore)
Tables: `person` (name, short name; UK UTR/NI and PT NIF optional, stored locally), `property` (name, country, matrix article, purchase price/date optional, notes), `propertyOwner` (property, person, share %, from/to dates), `propertyAccount` (property, account, role: current/mortgage/deposit/other), `propertyCategoryGroup` (property ↔ category group, e.g. "Lisbon"), `tenancy` (property, reference label e.g. "Quarto Contrato JDCR", contract no., tenant name, start/end, rent history rows {from, monthly amount}, advance rent months, deposit months, notes), `rentReceipt` (tenancy, receipt no., rent period start/end, receipt date, amount, withholding, amount received, status issued/cancelled, unique (tenancy contract no., receipt no.)). Evidence entity types extended (property, tenancy, rentReceipt, person) with delete triggers. APIs for CRUD + ownership share at a date. Commit "Property: people, properties, owners, tenancies, rent receipts".

### Task 2: Rent receipts import (BudgetCore)
Read the Portal das Finanças "ListaRecibos" XLSX (columns: Referência, Nº de Contrato, Nº de Recibo, Locador, Locatário, Data de Início, Data de Fim, Data de Rec., Valor (€), Retenção IRS (€), Importância recebida (€), Imóvel, Estado). Use a small, well-maintained XLSX reader via SPM (e.g. CoreXLSX) or a minimal ZIP+XML reader — must work on iOS too. Create/merge tenancies by contract no. (reference label, tenant, start = first period start, end = last period end if no later receipts), upsert receipts idempotently (re-import updates status, never duplicates), report new/updated/cancelled. Matching: link each receipt to the BBVA rent credit(s) that paid it (Rental income category on the property's current account; credit date within the period month ±10 days; many-to-one allowed; tenant transfers may exceed rent because of utility recharges — record the difference as "recharge/other" on the link); unmatched lists both ways. Tests with a synthetic XLSX. Commit "Property: rent receipts import and matching".

### Task 3: Property money, calendar and checks (BudgetCore)
`PropertyFinance.summary(property, period: calendar year | UK tax year | custom)`: rent per receipts (by rent period) vs rent received (BBVA credits), recharges (credits − rent), costs by the property's categories (converted to GBP and native EUR), mortgage interest vs capital (from the mortgage account: interest rows vs transfer legs), net cash flow, outstanding loan, deposit held, yield on purchase price if set; monthly series for charts. `PropertyCalendar`: obligations derived from recurring transactions + fixed rules (IMI May/Aug/Nov, PT IRS by end of June, UK SA 31 Jan / 31 Jul, mortgage rate resets quarterly, insurance renewals from cover periods). `PropertyChecks`: rent months without a receipt, receipts without a matched credit, credits without a receipt, deposit balance vs tenancy terms, expected recurring costs missing. Missing-evidence rules: one PDF per rent receipt (kind rentReceipt), one contract document per tenancy. Tests. Commit "Property: finance summary, calendar and checks".

### Task 4: Property UI (App)
Sidebar "Property" section listing properties → page with tabs: Overview (key figures, charts, outstanding loan, deposit, yield), Tenancies (list, rent history, receipts table with status, matched credit, evidence paperclip; import receipts…), Money (period picker calendar/UK tax year; table + charts; drill-down to transactions), Calendar, Checks (+ link to Missing evidence filtered to the property), Profile (owners & shares, linked accounts and category group, documents). Import receipts sheet: pick XLSX, preview counts, apply. Commit "Property screens".
